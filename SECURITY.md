# FreeUK Genealogy Security Reporting Policy

1. We encourage security experts to find bugs, vulnerabilities and privacy issues in our websites, servers or other systems, and report them to [security@freeukgenealogy.org.uk](mailto:security@freeukgenealogy.org.uk). We try to respond to all reports with 7 days.  
2. Currently, we do not have a bug bounty program since we are a charity, and need our limited income to achieve our aims.  
3. If you have found a bug, vulnerability or privacy issue please email us about it at the above address. Please outline the nature of the problem, how we can reproduce it and what you think is its impact. If you want to provide us with legally sensitive or personal information of our users then we will provide you with a way to get it to us on request.  
4. We ask that you do not make any issue public while we are working with you on it.  
5. In the course of your work please do not deliberately degrade our service. We do not consider denial of service attacks an interesting problem.  
6. Please do not acquire or expose personal data of our sites’ users beyond the minimum required to provide proof of concept.  
7. We consider email configuration out of scope of this policy.  
8. We don’t consider the names or versions of software we run to be sensitive information.  
9. We’re not responsible for issues you find in third party services we use. We will help you contact the relevant service owners if you wish.  
10. The determination if an issue does or does not fall within our policy is ours alone.  
11. We would be delighted if you were able to contribute a fix, or otherwise become a Tech Volunteer at Free UK Genealogy (see [https://github.com/FreeUKGen](https://github.com/FreeUKGen) for our projects and [https://www.freeukgenealogy.org.uk/about/volunteer/tech-volunteering-opportunities/](https://www.freeukgenealogy.org.uk/about/volunteer/tech-volunteering-opportunities/) for our opportunities).  
12. We are happy to thank you (anonymously if you prefer) on this page.   
13. We will also acknowledge the contribution of security expert volunteers in general in our Annual Report.
